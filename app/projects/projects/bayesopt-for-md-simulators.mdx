## The target data

Anhydrous glycerol, atmospheric pressure, 298.15 K.

| property | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| density | 1258.4 ± 0.86 kg/m³ | vibrating-tube densimetry, sample dried to 0.07 mass% water — Prieto et al., *J. Chem. Thermodyn.* 101 (2016), read from the NIST TRC ThermoML deposition; eight independent literature values cluster at 1257.8–1259.1 |
| enthalpy of vaporisation | 91 ± 1 kJ/mol | brackets Bastos et al., *J. Chem. Thermodyn.* 20 (1988), 91.7 ± 0.9 by direct vaporisation calorimetry, and Verevkin et al., *Fluid Phase Equilib.* 397 (2015), 90.5 ± 0.3 from evaluated vapour pressures |

The widely quoted 1261 kg/m³ is a 293.15 K value. Published ΔHvap figures of
85–86 kJ/mol are vapour-pressure work at 308–316 K never corrected to 298.15 K.

```
loss  =  0.5 · |ρ − ρ*| / ρ*   +   0.5 · |ΔHvap − ΔHvap*| / ΔHvap*
```

## The simulator

Purpose-built three-bead engine, not GROMACS or LAMMPS:

- glycerol mapped 3:1, one bead per C–OH unit, 30.698 u each
- 125 molecules, cubic periodic box, non-bonded interactions excluded within a molecule
- velocity Verlet, Berendsen thermostat at 298.15 K, Berendsen barostat at 1 bar
- density is measured, not imposed: 240 ps equilibration before it stops drifting,
  then 60 ps production — about 76 s per simulation
- 154 of 336 swept points crash; everything above ε ≈ 5 is infeasible

## The experiment

**Bayesian optimisation** — one Gaussian Process over the scalar loss
(`scikit-optimize`, Matérn kernel, `gp_hedge` acquisition, 8 random warm-up points,
crashes reported as 10.0). 12 seeds × 40 simulations.

**LLM** — Claude Opus 5 proposes every parameter set, seeing the target values, the
bounds, and each previous run's parameters with its loss or its failure mode. No
surrogate. 4 seeds × 20 simulations, 80 calls, **$3.15**.
